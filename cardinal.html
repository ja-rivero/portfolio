Designed brand book for a Series A YC start-up, scaling a paper concept to help customers make better sales decisions.

Cardinal (YC 2026) · Designer · 1 week contract project

Context

Cardinal is an AI revenue platform built for sales and go-to-market teams, straight out of Y-Combinator. Founded by two-time YC founders, Cardinal was looking a fresh brand book to hand off to partners and design clients.

I touched on public-facing assets (ex. LinkedIn posts, billboards, posters), but also website tokens like typography, padding, color palette, and iconography.

Posters

Staying true to Cardinal’s torn paper/mache concept, I found ways to weave language into visual abstraction. Large shapes and focal points help onlookers read for our central message.

Design context

To help develop new design tokens for our website, I tested different (and more affordable) typography and color options.

Our team ended up preferring this blue wash, given its soothing hue and readability.

Product and web assets

These are replicable visuals to show impactful processes for users.

I selected modals that could potentially tell a story about our product: finding leads on customers and businesses.

Iconography/logo

I reimagined logo display and isolation — mainly focusing on geometric lines and paper-textured ridges while editing.

Value points and takeaways

  1. Really just start building ANYTHING. Creative blocks are not permanent and completely recoverable. Don’t sit around waiting too long and start chasing something.
  2. Don’t hesitate to show your work, even if it isn’t “your best”. Quality can be subjective and you never know what others will think.
